# Design System Override: Page V2 (Domus Voley Pro Edition)

> **Page**: `/v2` & `/index-2`
> **Archetype**: Pro Athletic Volleyball Club & Training Academy
> **Authentic Logo Palette**: Official Teal Green (`#109b8b`), Ocean Navy (`#015480` / `#0b496d`), Electric Cyan (`#00f2d4`), Championship Gold (`#ffb703`), Pure White (`#ffffff`).

- **Logo Teal Primary**: `#109b8b` / `#14b8a6`
- **Electric Cyan Highlight**: `#00f2d4` / `#2dd4bf`
- **Deep Ocean Navy Background**: `#03131e` / `#041a29` / `#0b496d`
- **Surface Card**: `rgba(4, 26, 41, 0.85)` with `backdrop-blur-xl` and `border-teal-500/20`
- **Championship Accent**: `#ffb703` / `#f59e0b` (for trophies, stars, and tournament badges)
- **Text Crisp White**: `#ffffff` (contrast > 12:1 against ocean navy)
- **Text Slate Light**: `#94a3b8` / `#cbd5e1`

## 2. Header Architecture (Executive Floating Island)
